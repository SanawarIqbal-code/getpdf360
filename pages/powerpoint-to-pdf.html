PowerPoint to PDF questions

How do I convert PowerPoint to PDF for free?

Select your .ppt or .pptx file in the box above. It converts straight away; then download your PDF. There is no sign-up and no cost.

Will animations and videos work?

No. A PDF is a set of still pages, so each slide is saved as it looks at the end of its animations. Videos and sounds are not included.

Will my fonts look the same?

Common fonts come through exactly. Unusual fonts that aren't embedded may be swapped for a close match.

What is the biggest file I can convert?

Files up to 20 MB, which covers most presentations, including ones with images.

What happens to my file?

It is uploaded over HTTPS, converted on our server in London and deleted as soon as the PDF is sent back to you.

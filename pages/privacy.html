Privacy policy

How we handle your files and information, in plain English.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

The short version

  • Files you convert on our server are deleted as soon as the result is sent back to you.
  • Several tools never upload your file at all: they run inside your browser.
  • We don't use cookies, ads or tracking, and we never sell or share your files or data.
  • You can contact us any time at hello@getpdf360.com.

Who we are

GetPDF360 (getpdf360.com) is run by Sanawar Iqbal, an individual based in the United Kingdom. For data protection law, Sanawar Iqbal is the "controller" responsible for your information. Contact: hello@getpdf360.com.

Your files

Tools that run in your browser

Merge PDF, Split PDF, JPG to PDF, PDF to JPG, Rotate PDF, Add page numbers work entirely on your own device. Your files are never sent to our server or anyone else's, so we never see them.

Tools that use our server

Word to PDF, PDF to Word, Compress PDF, PowerPoint to PDF, Excel to PDF need our server to do the conversion. For these tools:

  • Your file is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our server in London, United Kingdom.
  • It is converted automatically by software. No person opens or reads it.
  • Your file and the result are deleted as soon as the result is sent back to you. The privacy receipt on the page shows when this happened.
  • If something goes wrong part-way, an automatic clean-up deletes any leftover files within one hour.
  • We never keep copies, share your files, or use them to train software or AI.

Technical information

Like almost every website, our server automatically records basic technical details when you visit, such as your IP address, the page you asked for, the time, and your browser type. We use this only to keep the site secure, stop abuse and fix errors. It is not used for advertising or profiling, and it is deleted on a regular basis.

Cookies and tracking

GetPDF360 does not use cookies, advertising, or tracking tools. If this ever changes, we will update this page first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

Why we can use this information

We process your files and technical information because it is necessary to provide the service you asked for and to keep it secure. In data protection law this is called "legitimate interests".

Who else is involved

Our server is provided by DigitalOcean, in its London data centre. DigitalOcean provides the computer the site runs on; it does not use your files. We do not sell or give your information to anyone else, unless the law requires us to.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete information we hold about you, and you can object to how we use it. Because we delete files straight away and don't ask for your name or email, we usually hold nothing that identifies you. To make a request, email hello@getpdf360.com.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Children

GetPDF360 is a general tool and is not aimed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information about children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date at the top.

About GetPDF360

Free PDF tools that respect your privacy, built and run in the UK.

GetPDF360 is a free set of PDF tools built and run by Sanawar Iqbal, a web developer in the United Kingdom. It started with a simple question: why do so many free PDF sites ask you to sign up, limit you to two files a day, or keep your documents on their servers?

What makes GetPDF360 different

  • Private by design. Six of our tools run entirely in your browser, so your file never leaves your device. The rest delete your file the moment you download the result, and a privacy receipt shows you when.
  • Free, with no catches. No sign-up, no watermark and no daily limit.
  • Hosted in the UK. Files that need our server are processed in London, over an encrypted connection.
  • Open source. Anyone can check how it works: the code is on GitHub.

The tools

GetPDF360 currently offers 11 tools: Word to PDF, PDF to Word, Compress PDF, Merge PDF, Split PDF, JPG to PDF, PDF to JPG, PowerPoint to PDF, Excel to PDF, Rotate PDF, Page numbers. More are on the way.

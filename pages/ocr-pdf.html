OCR questions

What is OCR?

OCR (optical character recognition) reads the letters in a picture of a page, like a scan or a photo, and turns them into real text you can edit, copy and search.

How do I convert a scanned PDF to Word for free?

Choose the language and Word, then select your scanned PDF in the box above. Download the .docx file when it's ready. There is no sign-up and no cost.

Does it work with Urdu?

Yes. Choose Urdu and the tool reads Urdu text, and English words mixed in. Clear, printed text works best; handwriting is much harder to read.

How accurate is it?

Clear printed scans usually come out very well. Blurry photos, handwriting, unusual fonts and complex layouts may need some fixing afterwards.

Is there a page limit?

Up to 20 pages and 20 MB at a time. For longer documents, use Split PDF and do it in parts.

What happens to my file?

It is uploaded over HTTPS, read on our server and deleted as soon as the result is sent back to you.

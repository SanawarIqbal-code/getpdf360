Is GetPDF360 really free?

Yes. Every tool is free to use, with no sign-up, no watermark and no daily limit.

Are my files safe?

Seven tools (Merge, Split, JPG to PDF, PDF to JPG, Rotate, Page numbers and Edit PDF) run in your browser, so your file never leaves your device. The others send your file over HTTPS to our server and delete it as soon as the result is sent back, and a privacy receipt shows you when.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. Open a tool and use it.

Is there a file size limit?

Compress PDF takes files up to 100 MB, and other server tools up to 20 MB. Browser tools such as Split and Edit PDF allow up to 100 MB, because the work happens on your own device.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. GetPDF360 works in any modern browser on iPhone, Android, Windows and Mac. There is nothing to install.

Do you keep or share my documents?

No. We never keep copies, share your files or use them to train software. See our privacy policy for details.

Is GetPDF360 really free?

Yes. Every tool is free to use, with no sign-up, no watermark and no daily limit.

Are my files safe?

Six tools (Merge, Split, JPG to PDF, PDF to JPG, Rotate and Page numbers) run in your browser, so your file never leaves your device. The others send your file over HTTPS to our server in London and delete it as soon as the result is sent back, and a privacy receipt shows you when.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. Open a tool and use it.

Is there a file size limit?

Server tools take files up to 20 MB. Browser tools allow more, for example up to 100 MB for Split PDF, because the work happens on your own device.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. GetPDF360 works in any modern browser on iPhone, Android, Windows and Mac. There is nothing to install.

Do you keep or share my documents?

No. We never keep copies, share your files or use them to train software. See our privacy policy for details.

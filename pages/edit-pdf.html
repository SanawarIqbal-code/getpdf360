Edit PDF questions

How do I edit a PDF for free?

Select your PDF in the box above. Choose Text, White-out, Draw or Image, click on the page to add your change, then press Save PDF. There is no sign-up and no cost.

Can I change the existing text?

You can cover it. Use White-out to hide the words you want to change, then add new text on top. Editing the original words directly isn't possible yet.

How do I sign a PDF?

Choose Draw / sign and write your signature with your mouse or finger. Or choose Image and add a photo of your signature.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Editing runs entirely in your browser, on your own device. Your PDF never reaches our server.

Which languages can I type?

English letters, numbers and common symbols work now. Other scripts, like Urdu, aren't supported in the text tool yet.

Can I annotate a PDF online?

Yes. Add text notes, draw with the pen, cover words with white-out and add images or stamps, all online in your browser with nothing to install.

Rotate PDF questions

How do I rotate a PDF for free?

Select your PDF in the box above, choose the direction and which pages, then press Rotate. Download the result. There is no sign-up and no cost.

Can I rotate just one page?

Yes. Choose Only some pages and type the page number, for example 4, or several like 2, 5-7.

Is the rotation permanent?

Yes. The saved PDF opens with the pages turned in any PDF reader.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Rotation runs entirely in your browser, on your own device. Your PDF never reaches our server.

Will the quality change?

No. Pages are only turned, so text and images stay exactly the same.

    Merge PDF questions

    How do I merge PDF files for free?

    Select two or more PDFs in the box above, put them in order, then press Merge. Download your combined PDF. There is no sign-up and no cost.

    Are my files uploaded?

    No. Merging runs entirely in your browser, on your own device. Your PDFs never reach our server, so there is nothing to delete.

    Can I change the order of the files?

    Yes. Drag the files into the order you want, or use the up and down arrows. You can also remove a file or add more before merging.

    Will the quality change?

    No. Pages are copied exactly as they are, so text, images and links stay the same.

    Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

    Not yet. Remove the password first, then merge the files.

Split PDF questions

How do I split a PDF for free?

Select your PDF in the box above, choose Extract pages, Every page or Custom ranges, then download the result. There is no sign-up and no cost.

How do I type the pages I want?

Use commas and dashes. For example, 1-3, 5, 8-10 means pages 1 to 3, page 5 and pages 8 to 10.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Splitting runs entirely in your browser, on your own device. Your PDF never reaches our server.

Why do I get a zip file?

When you split into several PDFs, we put them in one zip file so you can download them all at once. Your computer or phone can open it with a tap.

Can I split a password-protected PDF?

Not yet. Remove the password first, then split the file.

Compress PDF questions

How do I compress a PDF for free?

Choose a level, then select your PDF in the box above. It compresses straight away; then download the smaller file. There is no sign-up and no cost.

How much smaller will my PDF be?

It depends on what is inside. PDFs with photos or scans often shrink a lot; PDFs that are mostly text are already small and shrink less. You see the exact saving after compressing.

Will the quality drop?

Text and lines stay sharp. Images are resized: Recommended looks good on screen, Strong is smallest but images look softer, and Light keeps the most detail.

What if my PDF can't get any smaller?

If compressing would not make it smaller, we give you back your original file unchanged, so you never get a bigger file.

Is it safe to upload my PDF?

Your upload is encrypted with HTTPS, processed on our server in London and deleted as soon as the smaller file is sent back to you.

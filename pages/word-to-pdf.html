Word to PDF questions

How do I convert Word to PDF for free?

Select your .doc or .docx file in the box above. It converts straight away; then download your PDF. There is no sign-up and no cost.

Will my formatting stay the same?

Yes, for almost all documents. If your file uses an unusual font that isn't embedded, it may be swapped for a close match.

Is it safe to upload my document?

Your upload is encrypted with HTTPS, processed on our server in London and deleted as soon as the PDF is sent back to you.

What is the biggest file I can convert?

Files up to 20 MB. That covers nearly every Word document, including ones with images.

Can I turn a PDF back into Word?

Yes. Use the swap button or open the PDF to Word converter.

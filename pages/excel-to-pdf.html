Excel to PDF questions

How do I convert Excel to PDF for free?

Select your .xls or .xlsx file in the box above. It converts straight away; then download your PDF. There is no sign-up and no cost.

Are all sheets included?

Yes. Every sheet that has content is added to the PDF, in order.

My table is cut across pages. How do I fix it?

In Excel, open Page Layout, set the orientation to Landscape and choose Fit to 1 page wide, save, then convert again.

Will formulas still work?

A PDF shows the results of your formulas as they were when saved, but it can't recalculate them.

What happens to my file?

It is uploaded over HTTPS, converted on our server in London and deleted as soon as the PDF is sent back to you.

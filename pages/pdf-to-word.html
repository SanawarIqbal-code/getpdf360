PDF to Word questions

How do I convert a PDF to Word for free?

Select your PDF in the box above. It converts straight away; then download your .docx file. There is no sign-up and no cost.

Can I edit the Word file?

Yes. You get a normal .docx file that opens in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, Pages and LibreOffice.

Does it work with scanned PDFs?

Not yet. A scanned PDF is a picture of the page, so it has no text to convert. It works with PDFs made from a computer, where you can select the text.

Will the layout be exactly the same?

Text, paragraphs and simple tables come through well. Complex layouts with many columns or text boxes may need small fixes afterwards.

What happens to my PDF?

It is uploaded over HTTPS, converted on our server in London and deleted as soon as the Word file is sent back to you.

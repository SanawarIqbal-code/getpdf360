Page number questions

How do I add page numbers to a PDF for free?

Select your PDF in the box above, choose the position and style, then press Add page numbers. Download the result. There is no sign-up and no cost.

Can I leave the cover page without a number?

Yes. Choose Skip the first page. The other pages keep their real page numbers, so page 2 shows 2.

Can I write "Page 1 of 10"?

Yes. Choose the Page 1 of 10 style to show the page number and the total.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. The numbers are added entirely in your browser, on your own device. Your PDF never reaches our server.

Will it cover my text?

Numbers sit in the page margin. If your document has text right at the edge, try a different position.

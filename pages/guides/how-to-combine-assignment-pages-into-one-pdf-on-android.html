How to combine assignment pages into one PDF on Android

Teacher wants one PDF, but you have ten photos? Here's how to combine your assignment pages into a single PDF on an Android phone, for free, with no app to install.

By Sanawar Iqbal · Updated 27 November 2026

If your pages are photos

  1. Open getpdf360.com/jpg-to-pdf in Chrome.
  2. Tap Select images and choose all your page photos from the Gallery or Files. You can select several at once.
  3. Use the arrows to put the pages in the right order.
  4. Choose A4 so every page is the same size.
  5. Tap Convert to PDF, then Download. The PDF goes to your Downloads folder.
JPG to PDFMade in your browser, so your photos never leave your phone.
Open JPG to PDF

If your pages are already separate PDFs

Use Merge PDF instead: select all the PDFs, put them in order and tap Merge PDFs.

Taking good photos of your pages

  • Lay each page flat in good light, and hold the phone straight above it.
  • Fill the screen with the page and keep your shadow off it.
  • Many Android phones can also scan documents: try the Scan button in the Google Drive app for neat, cropped pages.

Before you submit

  • A page sideways? Fix it with Rotate PDF.
  • File too big for the portal? Shrink it with Compress PDF.
  • Need page numbers? Use Add page numbers.

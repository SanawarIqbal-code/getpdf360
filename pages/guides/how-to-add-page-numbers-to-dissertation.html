How to add page numbers to your dissertation

Most universities ask for numbered pages. Here's how to add page numbers to your dissertation PDF in seconds, skip the title page, and check it before you submit.

By Sanawar Iqbal · Updated 12 November 2026

Check your university's rules first

Formatting rules vary, so check your handbook. Common requirements are:

  • numbers at the bottom centre or bottom right of each page;
  • no number on the title page, but it still counts as page 1; and
  • sometimes Roman numerals (i, ii, iii) for the pages before the main text.

Add page numbers to your PDF

  1. Open Add page numbers.
  2. Select your dissertation PDF.
  3. Choose the position: bottom centre, bottom right or top right.
  4. Choose the style: just the number, or "Page 1 of 40".
  5. Choose Skip the first page to leave the title page without a number. Page 2 will still show 2.
  6. Press Add page numbers and download.

Need Roman numerals for the front pages?

Our tool numbers pages 1, 2, 3. If your university wants i, ii, iii for the front pages, add the numbers in Word instead using Insert → Page Number with a section break after the front pages, then convert with Word to PDF.

Final checks before you submit

  • Scroll through every page to check the numbers don't cover any text.
  • Check the order: combine separate chapters first with Merge PDF.
  • Check the file size against your submission portal's limit; shrink it with Compress PDF if needed.
  • Keep a copy of the final version you submit.

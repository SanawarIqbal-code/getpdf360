How to convert lecture slides from PDF to PowerPoint

Teachers often share slides as PDFs. Here's how to turn them back into PowerPoint, so you can add your own notes, reuse slides in a group project, or present them.

By Sanawar Iqbal · Updated 27 November 2026

Choose the right slide type

  • Editable: text comes through as text boxes you can change. Best for adding notes or reusing content.
  • Exact look: each page becomes a picture slide that looks exactly like the PDF. Best for presenting as it is.

Convert your slides

  1. Open PDF to PowerPoint.
  2. Choose Editable or Exact look.
  3. Select the PDF of the slides.
  4. Download the .pptx file and open it in PowerPoint, Google Slides or Keynote.

Tips

  • Layout looks a bit off in Editable? Complex designs may need tidying. Try Exact look if you only need to present.
  • Slides are scanned or photographed? Use OCR PDF to get the text first.
  • Only need a few slides? Take those pages out first with Split PDF.
  • Using someone else's slides? Credit your teacher or the original author if you reuse them in your own work.

Adding notes for revision

In PowerPoint, open the Notes panel under each slide (View → Notes) and type your own explanations. It's a handy way to turn lecture slides into revision notes.

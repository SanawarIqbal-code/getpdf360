How to convert your CV to PDF

Recruiters prefer PDFs because they look the same on every device. Here are three free ways to turn your CV into a PDF, and how to check it before you apply.

By Sanawar Iqbal · Updated 12 November 2026

Why send your CV as a PDF?

  • Your layout stays fixed. Fonts, spacing and columns look exactly as you designed them, on any computer or phone.
  • No accidental edits. A PDF is much harder to change by mistake than a Word file.
  • Accepted everywhere. Almost every job site and recruiter accepts PDF. Only send Word if a job advert specifically asks for it.

Method 2: Save as PDF in Microsoft Word

  1. Open your CV in Word.
  2. Choose File → Save As (or Save a Copy).
  3. Pick PDF as the file type and save.

Method 3: Download as PDF from Google Docs

  1. Open your CV in Google Docs.
  2. Choose File → Download → PDF document (.pdf).

Check your CV before you send it

  • Open the PDF and check every page: fonts, bullet points and page breaks.
  • Name the file clearly, for example Jane-Smith-CV.pdf, not CV final v3.pdf.
  • Check the size. Most job sites accept files up to a few MB. If yours is bigger because of photos, use Compress PDF.
  • Make sure text can be selected. Some recruiter software reads the text in your CV, so avoid turning it into a picture.

How to combine PDF files on iPhone

Need to send several PDFs as one file from your phone? Here are two free ways to merge PDFs on an iPhone, with no app to download.

By Sanawar Iqbal · Updated 12 November 2026

Method 1: Merge in Safari with GetPDF360

Works on any iPhone, and lets you choose the order before merging. Your files never leave your phone: merging happens inside the browser.

  1. Open getpdf360.com/merge-pdf in Safari.
  2. Tap Select PDF files, then Browse to pick the PDFs from the Files app. Tap Select to choose several at once.
  3. Put them in order with the arrows.
  4. Tap Merge PDFs, then Download.
  5. Tap the download icon in Safari to open the file, then use Share → Save to Files or send it straight away.

Method 2: Use the Files app

Recent versions of iOS can combine files without any website.

  1. Open the Files app and go to the folder with your PDFs.
  2. Tap the ⋯ button, then Select, and choose your PDFs.
  3. Tap ⋯ at the bottom right and choose Create PDF.

The pages are combined in the order the files appear in the folder, so you may need to rename them first (for example 1-cover, 2-essay) to get the right order.

Tips

  • Got photos, not PDFs? Use JPG to PDF to turn them into one PDF first.
  • Merged file too large to send? Shrink it with Compress PDF.
  • Need to remove a page afterwards? Use Split PDF to keep just the pages you want.

How to make a PDF smaller for email

Got a "file too large" message? Here's why PDFs get big, how to shrink them for free in a few seconds, and what to do if a file is still too large.

By Sanawar Iqbal · Updated 12 November 2026

How big can an email attachment be?

Most email services limit attachments to around 20–25 MB in total, and many work email systems allow less. If your PDF is close to the limit, or several files together go over it, the email won't send.

Why are some PDFs so large?

  • Scans and photos. Every scanned page is a full image, which is the biggest cause of large PDFs.
  • High-resolution pictures straight from a phone camera.
  • Embedded fonts and duplicate images saved more than once.

PDFs that are mostly typed text are usually small already.

How to compress a PDF for free

  1. Open Compress PDF.
  2. Choose a level: Recommended suits most files, Strong gives the smallest file, Light keeps the best quality.
  3. Select your PDF. It compresses straight away and shows you how much smaller it is.
  4. Download the smaller file and attach it to your email.

Still too big?

  • Try the Strong level. Images look softer, but text stays sharp.
  • Split it into parts with Split PDF and send them in separate emails.
  • Remove pages you don't need using Split PDF → Extract pages.
  • Share a link instead using a cloud drive, if the file must stay full quality.

Will compressing ruin my PDF?

No. Text and lines stay sharp at every level. Only images are resized, and if a file can't get any smaller we give you back the original unchanged.

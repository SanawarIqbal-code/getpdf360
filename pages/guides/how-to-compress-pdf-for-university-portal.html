How to compress a PDF for a university upload portal

Upload rejected because the file is too large? Here's how to make your assignment, transcript or scanned document fit your university portal's limit, for free.

By Sanawar Iqbal · Updated 27 November 2026

First, find your portal's limit

The limit is usually shown next to the upload button or in the error message, for example "Maximum file size: 2 MB" or "Files must be under 500 KB". Admission and scholarship forms often have the smallest limits, sometimes 100 KB to 300 KB per document.

Compress to a size limit

  1. Open Compress PDF.
  2. Choose Size limit, then pick the limit your portal asks for, such as Under 500 KB or Under 1 MB.
  3. Select your PDF. We try gentle settings first and only go stronger if we need to.
  4. Download the result and check the new size before you upload.

Quick links for common limits: under 100 KB, under 200 KB, under 500 KB and under 1 MB.

Still too big?

  • Send only the pages they asked for. Use Split PDF to keep just those pages.
  • Rescan at a lower quality. A scan at 150–200 dpi is clear enough for most documents and much smaller than 600 dpi.
  • Scan in black and white if colour isn't needed. Greyscale pages are far smaller.
  • Avoid photos of pages where you can. A typed document saved as PDF is tiny compared with a photo.

Before you upload

  • Open the compressed PDF and zoom in: check names, numbers and signatures are still easy to read.
  • Name the file clearly, for example Ali-Khan-Transcript.pdf.
  • Keep the original, full-quality file somewhere safe.
